# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. Before Release 1 the major number stays 0: a MINOR bump marks a stage the Product
Owner has approved, a PATCH bump marks any other release-worthy merge. 1.0.0 is Release 1. Version changes only in a
small release pull request that edits `package.json` and this file; the tag `vX.Y.Z` goes on the merge commit only when
the Product Owner says so. Every build also carries its short commit, build time and channel (Home footer, tap the
version for About).

## 0.6.0 - 2026-11-25

Bad Moon Rising begins: the Pacifist, the Sailor, the Exorcist and the Zombuul are guided.
- Zombuul (BM-2, #53): his first death only registers. The table sees him die and he secretly lives on, known only to the
  Storyteller's own board. He does not wake after a Day death, and his second death is real. Includes his official DIED TODAY
  disc.
- Pacifist, Sailor and Exorcist (BM-1, #52).
- Pit-Hag (#51): tap a player, choose the new character from the token picker, and Kill or Protect on nights when deaths are
  arbitrary.
- Night cards (#50): Begin Day moves to the card header, Previous and Next become chevrons at the ends of the strip, and the
  card buttons follow one layout.

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-24

First numbered version. Trouble Brewing complete; Sects & Violets fully built and validated by the SV-6 sweep (the live
game is still pending); Bad Moon Rising, Travellers, Fabled, Loric and Experimental characters still to come.

## Rights status (read before redistributing)
- They were collected for the owner's **private personal use** under TPI's Community Created Content Policy
  (https://bloodontheclocktower.com/pages/community-created-content-policy), "You can make what you like for personal use".
  That policy is not a licence. **Public release, redistribution and commercial use were not cleared** by the collection.
- Decision (2026-10-15): the project owner (Guillermo) chose to include these files in this repository after being told that a
  public repository makes them downloadable by anyone. Anyone reusing this repository's art must reassess TPI's current terms.
- This file is the removal manifest: deleting `public/art/` (the app falls back to marked temporary treatments) removes all of it.

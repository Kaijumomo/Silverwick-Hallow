# Sects & Violets SV-1: pair record for the Sage, Sweetheart, Klutz and Barber

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-yltpht` from main `9de844c`. In the style of the [Trouble Brewing pair record](TROUBLE_BREWING_B9_PAIRS.md): each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose). It becomes the first part of the script's record for SV-6.

Scope: the four SV-1 characters against the other 21 characters of Sects & Violets (84 pairs) and against each other (6 pairs): 90 unordered pairs, no self-pairs. This is not 90 dedicated tests. The rules come from the saved wiki pages of 2026-11-11 (Sage rev 3009, Sweetheart rev 2704, Klutz rev 1758, Barber rev 1757).

Two facts carry many rows:

- **The reviewed-list gate.** Five characters of the script are not on the reviewed list yet: the Snake Charmer, Philosopher, Evil Twin, Cerenovus and Fang Gu. While any of them is in play, every SV-1 step and Day row is the Storyteller's (Manual), as for every other guided character. Tested for all 20 pairs in one scenario.
- **The shared impairment query.** The Sweetheart's DRUNK is an Effect read by the same query as every other drunk or poisoned source, so every character she makes drunk stops having a working ability, and the Storyteller answers its information as today.

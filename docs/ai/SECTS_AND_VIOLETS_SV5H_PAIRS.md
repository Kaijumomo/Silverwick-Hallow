# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 5: pair record for the endings

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-x4i9rg` from main `2a0a9de`. Each Sects & Violets character against the ending rules (the Demon's death, two players alive, the Evil Twin's block, the Klutz, the Vortox, the Mastermind's final day): **25 rows**, one per character. Same assessment words as the other pair records.

The rules come from plan v3 ("all 25 S&V at Level B: the app proposes, the Storyteller confirms") and the PO's answers of 2026-11-23: Q19 A (the Evil Twin, Klutz and Vortox lines keep "Check before ending the game"; only the Demon-death and two-alive routes state the winner plainly) and Q20 A (the first Demon-death proof covers Day executions and the Slayer's shot only).

Facts that carry many rows:

- **The list.** All 25 characters are on the reviewed game-ending list (`victoryEvidence.ts` `supportedRoles`, mirrored in `coverageEvidence.ts`). Any character outside Trouble Brewing and Sects & Violets, any Fabled or Loric, the Pit-Hag's arbitrary deaths, the Toymaker and the Mastermind's final day still keep the ending manual.
- **The proof.** "The Demon is dead. Good wins" needs a recorded proof made at the death: the Imp on Day and Night as before, and the four Sects & Violets Demons on the Day only. Scarlet Woman succession, a second Demon and the Evil Twin's block keep their rules.
- **Never an ending.** No winner is recorded or preselected; the Storyteller ends the game.
- **No stored-format change.** The proof's stored shape is the same; it names the dead Demon as its source character.

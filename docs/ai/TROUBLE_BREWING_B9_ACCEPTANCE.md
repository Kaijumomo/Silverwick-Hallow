## Classification

- **D — deterministic:** source and test assertions support the bounded automation described. Player choices remain player choices; a deterministic effect does not mean a computer chooses the target.
- **S — intentional decision/manual boundary:** Storyteller discretion, player voting responsibility, direct physical Grimoire viewing, approved information selection or unsupported-history adjudication. These must preserve the existing controls and ordinary Next behavior.
- **G — unresolved deterministic/verification gap:** implementation or required acceptance evidence is missing. Do not reclassify it as intentional manual play merely because a Storyteller can compensate.

Every row separates D and S and states any current G. Test references are executable assertions inspected in source; their final execution status comes from exact-candidate logs. Prior B8 independent evidence is historical for its SHA, not an independent run of every test in this B9 audit.

## Reusable invariants and reminder acceptance

Information uses the existing authoritative delivery/preparation records, never token text as truth. Effects retain exact sources and participant identity even when equivalent visible reminders group. Permanent source loss differs from temporary impairment. Life distinguishes execution, death, resurrection and correction; ability use and Night consumption stay separate from visible No Ability notation. Setup effects do not replay after live character changes. Ending assistance is a derived hint, not a second winner record.

Keep the approved two-tap, three-second removal interaction and Undo as the primary correction path. Generated clue/reminder links retire only their exact instances; existing manual notation remains. Corrections never retroactively mint succession, information or temporal ending evidence. Relevant suites include [reminder architecture](../../src/stores/reminderArchitecture.test.ts), [Night correction](../../src/stores/nightActionCorrection.test.ts), [B2 UI](../../src/features/nightOrder/troubleBrewingB2Independent.test.tsx), [B6 linkage](../../src/stores/b6Independent.test.ts) and [B7 use/removal](../../src/stores/b7IndependentSlayer.test.ts).

# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 3: pair record for the Vortox

> **History.** The final truth for every pair of the Sects & Violets script is in [SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md) (SV-6a). This record is kept as it was written; its rows are not edited further.

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-aaxed3` from main `d19c7b9`; updated after the independent check. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) to [SV-5 part 1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV5A_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Vortox against every other character of Sects & Violets: **24 unordered pairs**. Each row is also the Vortox row of the other character (the SV-5 part 1 rows 024, 047, 069 and 090 now point here). The rules come from the saved Vortox page ("Each night\*, choose a player: they die. Townsfolk abilities yield false info. Each day, if no-one is executed, evil wins.") and the PO's answers of 2026-11-22: Q9 A (the seven guided information characters get false answers), Q10 A (the Storyteller chooses among the answers that are not true; a Yes / No is flipped automatically), Q11 A (a guided kill step), and his vision of the same day (the no-execution ending is a line on the End control; the Storyteller confirms).

Five facts carry many rows:

- **One shared rule.** A Townsfolk ability's information is false while any living, sober and healthy canonical Vortox is in play (`townsfolkInformationFalse`). Each guided information character declares its truth once; the coordinator refuses a true answer (`falseInformationRefusal`). An answer is "true" only when it is true under every registration a Spy or Recluse is allowed, so a Spy or Recluse never forces a by-hand step. A drunk or poisoned Townsfolk still gets false information; a Drunk only shown a Townsfolk has no Townsfolk ability (the Storyteller's free choice).
- **Unknown stays by hand.** A Vortox whose sobriety is the Storyteller's judgment, and any Townsfolk information step that has not declared its truth (Savant, Artist, most Philosopher-gained abilities; the Juggler until SV-5f and the Mathematician until SV-5g declared their truth), stays wholly by hand through the existing information modifier.
- **The kill.** The Imp's ordinary Demon kill, with no star-pass, through the shared death gate: protection, the Fool, a Pit-Hag's arbitrary deaths and two living Demons behave exactly as for the other Demons. The Vortox's DEAD token; its cause recorded.
- **The no-execution ending.** The Vortox's state at the end of the Day (alive, sober and healthy, or in doubt) is captured at the exact Day-to-Night boundary from the authoritative state, because a Poisoner's poison expires at dusk. Whether anyone was executed is read live from the Day's execution record, so an execution recorded late takes the line away and a retracted one brings it back. Any execution outcome counts (died, survived, already dead, the Mutant, an ability execution); an exile or a non-execution death (the Witch) does not. One advisory line on the End control and in the End dialog's "Possible game end" box through that Night; hidden in Privacy Mode; the Storyteller ends the game. What happens to the Vortox after dusk does not change that Day's ending.
- **No stored-format change.** Two new registered capture-only game-rule fact types (the Klutz precedent); no schema version, Firebase Rules or migration change.

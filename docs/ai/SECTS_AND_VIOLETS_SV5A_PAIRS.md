# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 1: pair record for the Clockmaker, the Seamstress, the Dreamer and the Flowergirl

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-u7vzpe` from main `e9e6062`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) to [SV-4](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV4_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: each of the four against every other character of Sects & Violets, each pair counted once: 24 + 23 + 22 + 21 = **90 unordered pairs**. The rules come from the saved wiki pages (Clockmaker, Seamstress, Dreamer, Flowergirl, saved 2026-11-11) and the PO's answers of 2026-11-21: Q1 numbers and Yes / No stay on the Storyteller's card for a finger or nod signal; Q2 Spy and Recluse registration follows the pages (automatic when every allowed registration gives the same answer, otherwise one Storyteller question offering only the possible answers); Q3 the Flowergirl's tokens are derived from the voting record, nothing new stored; Q4 the Dreamer's tokens are shown good first, evil second.

Five facts carry many rows:

- **The reviewed list.** All four were already on it (SV-3), so none of them gates another character's action, and every other character of the script is on it.
- **Impairment.** A drunk or poisoned actor (the No Dashii's neighbour, the Vigormortis's, the Sweetheart's drunk, the real holder of a character a Philosopher chose) makes the Storyteller choose the answer; the Seamstress's pointing still uses her ability. One shared functioning query, tested per character with poison and drunkenness.
- **Changed characters.** The Clockmaker, the Seamstress and the Dreamer read the table as it is when they wake (swaps, jumps and Pit-Hag changes included). The Flowergirl reads the Day's votes: a voter whose character changed since that Day began may have been a Demon then, so the Storyteller answers one Yes / No question; the app never guesses.
- **The Vortox.** Every Townsfolk information step is by hand while a Vortox is in play, through the shared information modifier, until the Vortox build (SV-5 part 2).
- **The Philosopher.** A Philosopher's gained Clockmaker, Seamstress, Dreamer or Flowergirl ability is the Storyteller's, by hand (SV-3b runs only the Snake Charmer, the Town Crier and the Oracle). The gained Flowergirl still gets her derived tokens, as the Town Crier's.

# Sects & Violets: the master pair record (all 300 pairs)

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-80qkg9` from main `d2abdcb` (SV-6a). **This file is the final truth for every pair of the Sects & Violets script.** The nine older records (`SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md` to `SV5G_PAIRS.md`) were written stage by stage and stay as history; each now carries a pointer here, and their rows are not edited further. The Mastermind's pairs (`SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV5D_PAIRS.md`) belong to Bad Moon Rising and stay in their own record.

In the style of the [Trouble Brewing pair record](TROUBLE_BREWING_B9_PAIRS.md): exactly 25 characters, 25 x 24 / 2 = **300 unordered pairs, each once, no self-pairs**, in script order. Rules come from the saved wiki pages of 2026-11-11 (the re-check is `planning/sv6/WIKI_COMPARISON.md`, SV-6b).

This is a source and rules review, **not 300 executed pair tests**. The assessment words are the ones of the older records:

- **test**: a focused test names both characters, or the shared rule is tested with one of them. The Evidence column names it.
- **code read**: the shared query or command was read and both characters pass through it unchanged. The Evidence column names the file read.
- **Storyteller judgment**: the app leaves a decision open, and the row says what and why, so the Storyteller's attention is not spent finding out what the app will not do.

No row says "works" without one of the three. `src/abilities/characters/sectsAndVioletsSv6Pairs.test.ts` keeps the record honest (300 rows, every pair once, every cited file exists, every "test" row cites a test file); it checks the record, not the game.

## How the rows were made

- The nine records held 439 rows covering 285 of the 300 pairs (154 pairs sit in two or more records). **For each pair the record of the newest-built character wins**, because that stage re-read the pair; **273 rows are carried over unchanged** (the **From** column names the source row, for example `SV5G-012`).
- **12 rows were rewritten**. Eleven had gone stale: SV-1 and SV-2 said the Town Crier's and Oracle's answers "stay with the Storyteller", but both have been guided since the Treacherous 30th work (the Town Crier from the Day's recorded nominations, the Oracle from the dead players' registered alignments; the Storyteller chooses only when the character is drunk, poisoned or a Vortox is in play, or a Spy or Recluse registration is open). The twelfth (Evil Twin / Mathematician) was a "code read" that named no file; it now does.
- **15 rows are new**, for the pairs no stage owned (all among the No Dashii, Oracle, Pit-Hag, Town Crier, Vigormortis and Witch). Each is backed by a test in `sectsAndVioletsSv6GapPairs.test.ts`.
- Rows that say a gained or Pit-Hag-made ability "stays by hand" are about characters the app does not run on that path (the Philosopher's gained abilities other than three, the Savant and Artist information); they are still true.

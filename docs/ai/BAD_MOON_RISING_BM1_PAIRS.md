# Bad Moon Rising BM-1: pair record for the Sailor, the Exorcist and the Pacifist

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-h1jr01` from main `f48cc29`. In the style of the Sects & Violets pair records ([SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md)): each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose). A row marked **test** names its scenario (T1, T2, ...) in [bm1Pairs.test.ts](../../src/abilities/characters/bm1Pairs.test.ts).

Scope: each of the three against the other 69 characters of the Trouble Brewing (22), Sects & Violets (25) and Bad Moon Rising (22 others) scripts, plus the three among themselves: **210 unordered pairs**. Rows run Sailor, Exorcist, Pacifist, each against the other characters in script order.

Facts that carry many rows:

- **The Sailor.** One sourced `drunk` Effect (official DRUNK token) on the Sailor or on the chosen player, ruled by the Storyteller on one compact card (nothing pre-selected). It ends at dusk, or at once if the Sailor dies, becomes drunk or poisoned, or changes character. A sober, healthy Sailor cannot die from any cause the app knows (the Assassin is not built). Every other ability reads the one shared impairment query, so a Sailor-drunk character needs no character-by-character code.
- **The Exorcist.** One source-free `exorcistChosen` fact (official CHOSEN token) on the chosen player; it moves to the new pick and survives the Exorcist's death. Last night's player is refused. A working Exorcist that chose a player who registers as the Demon tells the Demon (the Wake row, the existing player-safe reveal) and the Demon's ordinary night wake does not happen, by one shared rule in the planner, never in a Demon's own file. Lasting powers, dusk steps and triggered wakes are untouched.
- **The Pacifist.** The execution question ("Does X die?", Dies / Survives) appears only for a good-or-unknown executed player while a living, certainly sober Pacifist exists, and never says the Pacifist caused it. When the rules already settle a survival with no ability spent, Execute records "survived" by itself.
- **Not on the reviewed list.** Twelve Bad Moon Rising characters are still not on the reviewed list (Grandmother, Gossip, Courtier, Minstrel, Tinker, Goon, Lunatic, Assassin, Mastermind, Zombuul, Pukka, Po). A game containing one of them stays wholly by hand, so those rows are **Storyteller judgment**: they say what the pages describe and what is left to the Storyteller, never that the app resolves it.
- **No stored-format change.** One new Effect type (`exorcistChosen`) with parameters the stored-effect schema already accepts; no schema version, migration, game-rule fact or Firebase Rules change.

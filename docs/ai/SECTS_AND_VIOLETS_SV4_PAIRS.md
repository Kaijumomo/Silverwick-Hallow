# Sects & Violets SV-4: pair record for the Cerenovus and the Evil Twin

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-0v16hn` from main `2114c9b`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md), [SV-2](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV2_PAIRS.md) and [SV-3](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV3_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Cerenovus against the other 24 characters of Sects & Violets (24 pairs) and the Evil Twin against the other 23 (the Cerenovus / Evil Twin pair counted once): **47 unordered pairs**. The rules come from the saved wiki pages (Cerenovus, Evil Twin, Mutant) and the PO's answers of 2026-11-21: Q1 night madness execution is an ordinary death plus a toast; Q2 a Day madness execution is recorded and the dock offers Begin Night; Q3 MAD applies only while the Cerenovus functions (a Vigormortis-kept Cerenovus still counts); Q4 the Evil Twin ending is an advisory line on the End control plus a block on good wins while both Twins live, and the Storyteller declares the winner; Q5 the Evil Twin is shown the Good Twin's true character; Q6 the Mutant's execute-at-any-time ships with the same Execute item.

Six facts carry many rows:

- **The reviewed list.** Both characters joined it (the Cerenovus in SV-4a, the Evil Twin in SV-4c), so every character of Sects & Violets is on it and nothing gates a Night action. The fourteen earlier pair rows that said "Manual: not on the reviewed list" (SV-1 014, 016, 035, 037, 056, 058, 077, 079; SV-2 017, 019, 040, 042; SV-3 017, 019) were rewritten as "Guided since SV-4a/SV-4c" with their gate tests flipped: a deliberate edit, not a regression.
- **MAD.** One sourced Effect on the chosen player with the madness character, shown as the official MAD token. It applies only while its Cerenovus functions (alive, sober and healthy, or killed by a Vigormortis), is derived live and never rewritten, and ends with the Day after the following night or with the Cerenovus's death or change of character (the shared source-ability loss).
- **Execute.** One seat-menu item (a mad player, a living Mutant). In the Day it records an ordinary execution (the Saint capture, the Undertaker's reading and the Day's execution count stay right; the existing "Did they die?" question appears where the rules gate says survival is possible; a second execution asks the existing confirmation). At night it is an ordinary death, with a reminder line for a Saint or a Good Twin.
- **The pair.** Read from the TWIN reminder (a source-free Storyteller fact naming the Evil Twin, placed on a good player at setup) and the two players' current alignments: valid while opposing, the good-aligned one is the Good Twin. It is never stored as a pair and the marker is never removed by itself; a broken pair or a Pit-Hag-made Evil Twin sends the Storyteller back to choose a new Good Twin by tapping the board (one Undo).
- **Endings.** `supportedRoles` gained both characters only together with the block: no route proposes "good wins" (the Demon's death, the Mayor's condition, an evil Saint's execution, an evil Klutz's choice) while a valid pair both live and the Evil Twin functions; the same routes stay proposed when the Evil Twin is dead (even Vigormortis-kept), impaired, or the Good Twin is dead, and go to the Storyteller when the pair is broken or the impairment is a judgment. The evil-win routes are unchanged. A game with any other Sects & Violets character beyond these two still keeps every ending proposal by hand.
- **Privacy.** Every line that names a Twin or the madness is hidden in Privacy Mode; neither the public projection nor a player's own carries MAD, the TWIN reminder, the pair or the madness character.

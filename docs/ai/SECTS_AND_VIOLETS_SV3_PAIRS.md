# Sects & Violets SV-3: pair record for the Philosopher

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-huqrw7` from main `0a7f8da`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) and [SV-2](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV2_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Philosopher against the other 24 characters of Sects & Violets: 24 unordered pairs. The six SV-1 and SV-2 rows that named the Philosopher (SV-1 010, 031, 052, 073; SV-2 009, 032) were rewritten in SV-3a and agree with this record. The rules come from the saved wiki page (Philosopher, revision 2421) and the PO's answers of 2026-11-21 (Q1 follow the page; Q2 the gained row runs as the real character's step; Q3 commit on pick; Q4 a fresh, poisoned Philosopher after a Snake Charmer swap; Q5 the true character decides who is "in play").

Five facts carry many rows:

- **The reviewed-list gate.** Every character of the script is on the reviewed list since SV-4c (the Cerenovus joined in SV-4a, the Evil Twin in SV-4c), so nothing gates the Philosopher's choice. With any of the other 24 characters in play the choice is guided (one scenario).
- **The chosen character is drunk, derived.** Whoever truly holds the chosen character (actual character: a Drunk or Marionette who only believes they are it does not count) is drunk while the Philosopher lives and is sober, with the Philosopher's official DRUNK token. Nobody truly holding it: the Storyteller's board shows the chosen character's token on the Philosopher's seat with IS THE PHILOSOPHER, and nobody is drunk. Tested for all 16 good characters of the script.
- **The gained row.** The gained ability wakes at its character's place: from the next night for an ability that wakes each night, the night it is gained for a first-night ability, the night after the first Day for the Juggler, and only after the Philosopher's death for an ability that works after death. A Day, passive or death ability has no night row. Tested for all 16.
- **What the app runs.** A gained Snake Charmer, Town Crier or Oracle runs exactly as the real character's step (SV-3b). Every other gained ability is the Storyteller's, by hand, at its place; the row says "Gained from the Philosopher." A gained once-per-game ability (Seamstress, Artist, Klutz) is the Storyteller's: the Philosopher's single used flag was spent by the choice itself.
- **Other scripts.** On a custom script, a gained character outside these 16 (a Soldier, Mayor, Virgin, Fool, Tea Lady, Monk, ...) sends every action to the Storyteller (Manual) while it is held, because the engine reads passive and life rules from a player's own character (independent check, finding 1).
- **Impairment.** A drunk or poisoned Philosopher (from the Sweetheart, the No Dashii, the Vigormortis, a Snake Charmer's poison or a hand-placed Effect) who chooses spends the ability and gains nothing; a Philosopher who becomes drunk or poisoned later keeps the gained ability (it does not work while impaired) and the drunk player is sober meanwhile.

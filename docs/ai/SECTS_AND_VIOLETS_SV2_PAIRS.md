# Sects & Violets SV-2: pair record for the Snake Charmer and Fang Gu

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-79ipjd` from main `8de9771`. In the style of the [SV-1 pair record](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md): each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose). Together with the SV-1 record it becomes part of the script's record for SV-6.

Scope: the Snake Charmer and the Fang Gu each against the other 23 characters of Sects & Violets (46 pairs) and against each other (1 pair): 47 unordered pairs, no self-pairs. The four SV-1 characters appear here from the SV-2 side; SV-1 rows 003, 018, 024, 039, 045, 060, 066 and 081 now point here. This is not 47 dedicated tests. The rules come from the saved wiki pages of 2026-11-11 (Snake Charmer, Fang Gu rev 3175).

Four facts carry many rows:

- **The reviewed-list gate.** Every character of the script is on the reviewed list since SV-4c (the Cerenovus joined in SV-4a, the Evil Twin in SV-4c), so nothing gates either SV-2 step any more. Tested: with each of the other characters in play, including the Philosopher since SV-3a, the Cerenovus and the Evil Twin, both steps are guided (one scenario).
- **The shared impairment query.** A drunk or poisoned Snake Charmer or Fang Gu (from the Sweetheart, the No Dashii, the Snake Charmer's own poison, or a hand-placed Effect) makes the choice and nothing happens.
- **ONCE on the board (PO 2026-11-21).** A jump places the official ONCE reminder beside the new Fang Gu, in the same commit. The reminder is a note; the game-rule fact is the truth. A jump resolved by hand records no fact by itself; the Storyteller places the Fang Gu's ONCE from the token list, and that placement records the fact in the same commit (PO decision 2026-11-21; one Undo; Night or Day). Removing a Fang Gu ONCE by hand clears the fact unless another Fang Gu ONCE is still on the board. Mechanics still read only the fact (test, fangGuUi.v6.test.tsx). With the fact recorded, an Outsider the Fang Gu kills dies as normal, and nothing is asked (test, fanggu.test.ts).
- **Information stays with the Storyteller.** The information characters of the script are answered by hand today. A swap or a jump changes who is the Demon and who is evil; those answers stay the Storyteller's, who sees the recorded characters and alignments.

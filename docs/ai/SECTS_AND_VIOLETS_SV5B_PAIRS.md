# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 2a: pair record for the Artist, the Savant and the Mutant

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-xj7eef` from main `d4cea32`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) to [SV-5 part 3](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV5C_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Artist, the Savant and the Mutant against every other character of Sects & Violets: **69 unordered pairs** (24 for the Artist, 23 more for the Savant, 22 more for the Mutant). The rules come from the saved Artist, Savant, Mutant and Cerenovus pages and the PO's answers: Q5 A (nothing typed), Q8 A (the Execute wording), Q12 A (the optional rows never raise the Day button's "!") and the build approval of 2026-11-23 (no "Ask again" pill).

Five facts carry many rows:

- **Real character only.** The Artist's and the Savant's rows read the actual character, alive, in the Day, outside Privacy Mode. A Drunk or Marionette who believes they are one, an evil bluffer and a Philosopher who gained the ability get no row (by hand). A drunk or poisoned real Artist or Savant keeps the row; the Artist's tap still spends the ability, and NO ABILITY follows from the use (the existing Life boundary notation).
- **What is recorded.** One tap is one manual resolution and one Undo: the Artist's answer (Yes, No or I don't know) or the Savant's "told today", as a Storyteller-only manual information delivery stamped with the Day. Nothing typed, never projected to a player or the Public Display.
- **Vortox.** Both rows carry the existing "Vortox · false info" pill while the shared rule applies (`townsfolkInformationFalse`, read live). The app cannot check a whispered answer, so the pill is the whole help.
- **One execution per day.** The seat's Execute (the Mutant's and a Cerenovus's mad player's, one function) records nothing once the Day has an execution and says "One execution per day: wait for tonight." At night it is unchanged (an ordinary death).
- **No stored-format change.** No new record type, field, Effect type, game-rule fact, schema version, Firebase Rules or migration change: manual information deliveries and the used flag already exist.

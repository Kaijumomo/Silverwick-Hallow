# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 2c: pair record for the Mathematician

> **History.** The final truth for every pair of the Sects & Violets script is in [SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_PAIRS.md) (SV-6a). This record is kept as it was written; its rows are not edited further.

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-0o1r99` from main `0bcf3ec`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) to [SV-5 part 2a](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV5B_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Mathematician against every other character of Sects & Violets: **24 unordered pairs**. The rules come from the saved Mathematician page (with its Chambermaid, Drunk, Lunatic and Marionette jinxes), the Vortox page's Mathematician example, and the PO's answers: Q1 A (finger signals on the Storyteller's card), Q7 A (a hand-placed official ABNORMAL token is a real mark), Q17 A (the app marks only the Vortox's false information and impaired information proven false) and Q18 A (marks clear at his commit; a later mark expires at dawn uncounted).

Six facts carry many rows:

- **The mark.** One `abnormal` Effect per affected player (a recorded Storyteller fact: no source, nothing depends on it functioning, no ending proposal turns manual). It is drawn with the official ABNORMAL token; its tooltip names the reason. A second occurrence in the same window adds its reason to the one mark. Storyteller-only: hidden in Privacy Mode, never projected to a phone or the Public Display.
- **By hand.** The Storyteller places the official ABNORMAL token from the token list on any player in a Night or a Day: that records the mark (no Reminder), one commit, one Undo. Removing the drawn token removes it. This is how every case below marked "Storyteller judgment" is counted.
- **By the app.** Only two cases, both in one coordinator step read for every descriptor, never per character: (a) the shared false-information rule applied (a living, sober, healthy Vortox): the Townsfolk who received the information is marked "Vortox: false information"; (b) a drunk or poisoned player's real ability delivered an answer its descriptor's truth proves false under every allowed registration: "False information while drunk or poisoned". A true answer under impairment, an answer a Spy or Recluse ruling could make true, a simulated wake (a Drunk, Marionette or Lunatic shown a character), the Mathematician's own number, and abilities with no declared truth (the Savant, the Artist, the Sage under impairment) are never marked by the app. Nothing is marked while no living player has the Mathematician's ability.
- **The count.** Every night (the first included) the Night card shows the number of players holding a mark, the Mathematician himself left out; Next records it and removes every mark in the same commit (one Undo restores both). Marks last until the next dawn (`untilDawn`), so a mark made after he woke is never counted.
- **Impaired, a Vortox, the jinxes.** A drunk or poisoned Mathematician, or a Drunk shown him: the Storyteller chooses 0 to 5. A Vortox: any number but the true count, with the existing "Vortox · false info" pill. With a Chambermaid, Drunk, Marionette or Lunatic in play the jinx sends his step to the Storyteller, by hand (none of the four is on Sects & Violets).
- **No stored-format change.** One new Effect type with a text parameter the stored-effect schema already accepts; no schema version, migration, game-rule fact or Firebase Rules change.

# Sects & Violets SV-5 part 2b: pair record for the Juggler

Built on branch `claude/project-thread-w7l9jh` from main `0bcf3ec`. In the style of the [SV-1](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV1_PAIRS.md) to [SV-5 part 2a](SECTS_AND_VIOLETS_SV5B_PAIRS.md) pair records: each pair is marked as **test** (a focused scenario names both, or the shared rule is tested with one of them), **code read** (the shared query or command was read, no dedicated scenario), or **Storyteller judgment** (the app leaves it by hand on purpose).

Scope: the Juggler against every other character of Sects & Violets: **24 unordered pairs**. Each row is also the Juggler row of the other character; where an earlier record says the Juggler's answers "stay with the Storyteller" (SV-1 012, 033, 075; SV-2 011, 034; SV-5 part 3 012), this record replaces it. The rules come from the saved Juggler page ("On your 1st day, publicly guess up to 5 players' characters. That night, you learn how many you got correct." How to Run: "For each correct guess, mark the Juggler with a CORRECT reminder ... That night, wake the Juggler. Show them fingers ... equaling the number of CORRECT reminders"), the proposal's sections 14.1 and 36 to 38, and the PO's answers Q6 A (a guess is judged when it is made), Q12 A (the optional Day rows never raise the Day button's "!") and Q16 A (mistakes are fixed with Undo, nothing typed).

Six facts carry many rows:

- **Real character, first Day.** The Day row is for a living real Juggler on their first Day as the Juggler, read by the Night order's own rule (`jugglerFirstDay`), in the Day, outside Privacy Mode and an ended game. A drunk or poisoned real Juggler keeps the row (the count is judged by their state at night: the page). A Drunk who believes they are the Juggler, an evil bluffer and a Philosopher who gained the ability get no row; their Night step stays by hand.
- **A guess is judged when made.** Guess: tap any seated player (the dead and the Juggler included), then pick a character from the board's token picker (tokens only). The guess is correct when the player registers as that character to the Juggler now: their actual character, or the Storyteller's one Yes / No for a healthy Spy or Recluse who might register as it (a form they cannot take is simply incorrect). One guess is one `jugglerGuess` Effect on the Juggler and one Undo; repeats are allowed; a sixth is refused; the toast never says whether it was right. Done is one more Effect (zero guesses allowed). A later character change of the guessed player does not change a guess already made.
- **The tokens.** Each correct guess draws one official CORRECT token on the Juggler in the Storyteller's Grimoire; incorrect guesses and Done draw nothing. Hidden in Privacy Mode; never projected to a player's phone or the Public Display.
- **That night.** A sober and healthy Juggler is shown the count of correct guesses (0 to 5) and Next removes the guesses and Done. Drunk or poisoned tonight (or a Drunk shown the Juggler): the Storyteller picks any number 0 to 5, and the records are left to expire at dawn (the coordinator refuses any change from an ability that is not working). Done not recorded: the Storyteller picks the number and the card says why. A working Vortox: any number but the true count (the true one is refused); without Done, wholly by hand. A dead Juggler does not wake ("if they are not killed beforehand"); the records expire at dawn. The Cannibal jinx (not on this script) is not built: by hand.
- **The records go with the character.** A Juggler whose character changes (a Pit-Hag, a Barber, the Fang Gu) loses the guesses and Done at once, so no CORRECT token is left on a non-Juggler; death does not remove them. Whoever becomes the Juggler has their own first Day (the character timing).
- **No stored-format change.** Two new Effect types, `jugglerGuess` and `jugglerGuessesDone`, registered as Storyteller facts (so ending proposals stay as automated as before) and valid in the existing saved-game schema; no schema version, record type, field, Firebase Rules or migration change.
